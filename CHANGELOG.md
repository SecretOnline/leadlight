### Known Issues

- Transparent blocks (like Water) are invisible when behind filled Window Frames when Improved Transparency is off.
  - This is fixed when turning on Improved Transparency in the video settings.

### Added

- Iron Window Frames
  - Comes in 6 shapes: Large, Horizontal, Vertical, Square, Cross, and Diamond.
  - Craft a Large frame with 8 Iron Nuggets in a ring.
  - Craft the other shapes with a Large frame and more Iron Nuggets in different shapes.
  - Use Cut Stained Glass Panes to a space in the frame, use an empty hand to empty a space.
- Cut Stained Glass Panes
  - Crafted using Stained Glass Panes in a Stonecutter.
